WCAG 2.2 EXPLAINED

The standard, in plain English.

WCAG 2.2 is the W3C's web accessibility standard — 86 success criteria across four principles. Level AA is the legal target referenced by
ADA Title II, the new DOJ rule, and Section 508.

Every criterion falls under one of four principles, known as POUR. Hit all four and your library website works for every patron — including the screen-reader user searching the catalog, the keyboard-only user reserving a meeting room, the colorblind user reading event flyers, and the patron with low vision navigating your homepage.

P
Perceivable
Alt text on book covers, captions on storytime videos, sufficient color contrast.
O
Operable
Full keyboard navigation through the catalog, no time-out traps in renewal forms.
U
Understandable
Clear labels on hold/place/reserve buttons, helpful error messages on hold queues.
R
Robust
Semantic HTML and ARIA so JAWS and NVDA read your catalog correctly.
AA
Target Level AA. Level A is the floor (and not enough for Title II). Level AAA is aspirational and rarely achievable for content-heavy library sites. The DOJ's 2024 rule explicitly cites WCAG 2.1 AA — the AA target maps cleanly to 2.2.

WHICH LAWS APPLY

Three frameworks. One compliance target.

Public libraries sit at the intersection of three WCAG legal frameworks for digital accessibility, each with different laws and different scopes. Accessrank for Libraries can guide you through all three.

FEDERAL CIVIL RIGHTS LAW

ADA Title II

Applies to: state and local government services — including all public libraries.

The Department of Justice's April 2024 final rule made WCAG 2.1 Level AA the explicit technical standard for state and local government websites. Libraries serving 50,000+ residents must comply by April 24, 2026; smaller systems have until April 26, 2027.

Target standard WCAG 2.1 AA
FEDERAL PROCUREMENT LAW

Section 508

Applies to: federal agencies and any ICT they fund, develop, or procure.

Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act applies directly to federal libraries (Library of Congress, agency libraries) and indirectly to any public library receiving IMLS, LSTA, or other federal grants for digital services. Library platform vendors selling to federal agencies must furnish a VPAT (Voluntary Product Accessibility Template) demonstrating conformance.

Target standard WCAG 2.0 AA
INTERNATIONAL TECHNICAL STANDARD

WCAG 2.2

Applies to: any website wanting current best-practice accessibility.

The W3C's most recent guidelines, released October 2023. Adds nine new success criteria over WCAG 2.1 — most notably around mobile usability, focus appearance, and dragging movements. Hitting WCAG 2.2 AA satisfies both Title II and Section 508 simultaneously, with room to spare.

Target standard WCAG 2.2 AA

Accessrank for Libraries audits and remediates against WCAG 2.2 AA — the highest of the three. If your library passes our audit, you satisfy all three frameworks at once.
